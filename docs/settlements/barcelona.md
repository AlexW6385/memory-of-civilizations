## 1. The real example

- **Landmark:** the Sagrada Família, begun in 1882, with Gaudí in charge from 1883. In 1910 the crypt and the apse walls stood, and the Nativity façade was rising. Its first bell tower was finished in 1925 [stated: Wikipedia "Sagrada Família"].
- **The Eixample:** Ildefons Cerdà's plan of 1859, a grid of blocks about 113 m square with chamfered corners [stated: Wikipedia "Eixample"].
- **Gaudí's houses:** Casa Milà (La Pedrera, 1906–1912) with its undulating stone front, and Casa Batlló (remodelled 1904–1906) on the Passeig de Gràcia [stated: Wikipedia "Casa Milà"; "Casa Batlló"].
- **Moment shown:** about 1910.

## 1. The real example

- **Landmark:** St Mark's Basilica, a Greek-cross church under five domes, with tall lead-covered outer shells and a west front of five arched portals with mosaics [stated: Wikipedia "St Mark's Basilica"].
- **The Campanile:** about 98.6 m high, of brick, with its belfry and a pyramidal spire topped by an angel [stated: Wikipedia "St Mark's Campanile"].
- **The Doge's Palace:** two storeys of arcades carrying a wall patterned in pink and white stone, facing the basin and the Piazzetta [stated: Wikipedia "Doge's Palace"].
- **The squares:** the Procuratie Vecchie line the north side of the piazza. The two columns of San Marco and San Todaro stand at the Piazzetta's waterfront [stated: Wikipedia "Piazza San Marco"].
- **Moment shown:** about 1500.

## 3. Orientation

The basilica faces west down the piazza; the Piazzetta opens south to the water. [stated]

## 4. The style

Brick and Istrian stone; marble and gold mosaic on the basilica; lead domes; arcades; houses plastered under tiled roofs. [stated]

## 5. The town

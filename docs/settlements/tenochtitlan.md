# Mexica Tenochtitlan, 1519 — dossier

Settlement id: `tenochtitlan` · Style: `aztec` · Region: the Valley of Mexico (Lake Texcoco) · Code: `src/settlements/tenochtitlan/`
Status of this dossier: L1–L2 (the Sacred Precinct inside its serpent wall: the Templo Mayor with its twin stairways and the shrines of Tlaloc and Huitzilopochtli, the round temple of Ehecatl-Quetzalcoatl, the temples of Tezcatlipoca and of the Sun, the ballcourt, the tzompantli, the House of the Eagles, the calmecac; the palace of Moctezuma; the causeway west; canals with canoes; the calpulli houses; chinampas at the island's edge; the lake; ground, planting, weather, music). ## 1. The real example

- **Landmark:** the Templo Mayor (Huey Teocalli), founded 1325, enlarged at least seven times to 1519 [stated: Studio Matrx]; the sixth temple dedicated 19 December 1487, the precinct walled with serpent heads [stated: Wikipedia; ancient-history-sites].
- **Moment shown:** 1519, the seventh temple, as Cortés saw it [stated: Wikipedia].

## 2. Layout of the settlement (radius 400 m)

| Part | Where (m, +x east, +z south) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sacred Precinct | in the middle, walled (coatepantli) | about 500 m square, gates toward the causeways [stated: ancient-history-sites; dragontravelmag]; 78 buildings by Sahagún [stated: Wikipedia] — ours 300 m square |
| Templo Mayor | on the precinct's east side, facing west | base about 100 × 80 m, height 42–45 m [stated: ancient-history-sites]; four sloped terraces, two stairways with balustrades ending in serpent heads [stated: Wikipedia; dragontravelmag]; Tlaloc's shrine on the left (north) facing it, Huitzilopochtli's on the right (south) [stated: Wikipedia] |
| Other buildings | the round temple of Ehecatl on the axis, the ballcourt, the calmecac, temples of Quetzalcoatl, Tezcatlipoca and the Sun [stated: dragontravelmag]; the House of the Eagle Warriors [stated: Wikipedia] |
| Palace of Moctezuma | south-east of the precinct | [inferred: beside the precinct, as recorded] |
| Causeway | west, to Tlacopan | [inferred: the island joined to the shore by causeways] |
| Town | calpulli houses on a grid of canals and streets; chinampas at the edge; the lake | [inferred] |

Ours: the Templo Mayor at true size, 45 m to the shrines' floor, the shrines about 8 m more; the precinct compressed. [inferred]

## 3. Orientation

The Templo Mayor faces west, its east–west axis aligned to a sunset in early April [stated: ancient-history-sites]; ours due west.

## 4. The style

Stucco over stone and rubble, painted; the Tlaloc shrine blue and white, Huitzilopochtli's red and white [inferred from the codices and the excavated earlier stages]; serpent heads at the stairs' feet and along the wall; flat-roofed houses of adobe and stone plastered white. [inferred]

## 5. The town

One-storey houses of adobe round patios, the canals as streets with canoes, footbridges; chinampa plots with maize and willows (ahuejote) at the edges. [inferred]

## 6. Ground and water

The island level a little above the lake; canals and the lake at the edge. (`ground.js`) [inferred]

## 7. Planting

Willows (ahuejote) along the chinampas and canals, maize fields; few trees in the centre. [inferred]

## 8. Sky and weather

High valley (2,240 m): clear dry-season skies, afternoon thunderclouds in the rains. Latitude 19.4° N. [inferred]

## 9. Sound

Drums (huehuetl, teponaztli), clay flutes and conch trumpets, rattles (a plucked string, the ney and a drone stand in). [inferred]

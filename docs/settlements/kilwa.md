## 1. The real example

- **Landmark:** the Great Mosque of Kilwa, extended in the early 14th century with a prayer hall of bays roofed by domes and vaults on pillars, and a great dome. It was built of coral stone in lime mortar [stated: Wikipedia "Great Mosque of Kilwa"]. Ibn Battuta visited Kilwa in 1331 [stated: Wikipedia "Kilwa Kisiwani"].
- **Husuni Kubwa:** the sultan's palace on a cliff above the sea, east of the town, with courts and an octagonal bathing pool, built in the early 14th century [stated: Wikipedia "Husuni Kubwa Palace"].
- **The town:** stone houses of coral rag on the island's north shore [stated: Wikipedia "Kilwa Kisiwani"].
- **Moment shown:** about 1330.

## 1. The real example

- **Landmark:** Etemenanki, the ziggurat of Marduk. Its base was about 91 m square, and its height is often given as about 91 m in seven stages, after the Esagila Tablet [stated: Wikipedia "Etemenanki"]. Esagila, Marduk's temple, stood just south of it [stated: Wikipedia "Esagila"].
- **The Ishtar Gate:** the eighth gate of the inner city, built about 575 BC under Nebuchadnezzar II, of glazed brick in blue with rows of aurochs and dragons. The Processional Way through it was lined with walls of glazed lions [stated: Wikipedia "Ishtar Gate"].
- **The city:** the inner city had double walls. The Southern Palace stood west of the Processional Way near the gate, and the Euphrates ran through the city [stated: Wikipedia "Babylon"].
- **Moment shown:** about 570 BC.

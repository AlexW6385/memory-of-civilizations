## 1. The real example

- **Landmark:** the terrace, about 455 m north–south by 300 m east–west and 14 m above the plain [stated: satyori; bananas-for-scale], part cut from and part built against the foot of Kuh-e Rahmat [stated: studiomatrx]. It is reached by a double stair of two flights [stated: universalis], about 7 m wide [stated: bananas-for-scale]. The Gate of All Nations stands at its head [stated: studiomatrx]. The Apadana had 72 columns of about 20 m [stated: geographyworlds, satyori], 8.65 m apart and 1.6 m thick [stated: peymanmeli]; Wikipedia gives 24 m. The Hall of a Hundred Columns is 70 × 70 m [stated: Wikipedia "Persian column"]. Column heights: about 17 m at the Gate, about 12 m in the Hundred Columns, 7 m in the Treasury, the Hadish and the Queen's Palace [stated: cgtrader text].
- **Moment shown:** about 480 BC, under Xerxes: the Apadana and the Hadish finished, the Hundred Columns begun (shown complete).

## 3. Orientation

The terrace's sides run roughly with the cardinal directions; the stair faces west over the plain. [stated: plans]

## 4. The style

Dark grey limestone for the terrace, stairs, columns and door frames; thick mudbrick walls, plastered; tall slender fluted columns with bell bases and double-protome capitals carrying cedar beams; flat earth roofs; stepped merlons on the parapets; winged human-headed bulls at the gate. [stated: columns, capitals; inferred: plaster colour, merlons on roofs]

## 5. The town

Flat-roofed mudbrick houses round small courts, with gardens of poplar, cypress and plane trees. [inferred]

## 6. Ground and water

The dry plain; the bare mountain behind; orchards and fields at the edge. [inferred]

## 7. Sky and climate

High and clear; spring clouds sometimes. [inferred]

## 8. Paths

From the town along the road to the stair, up either flight to the landing, back to the top, through the Gate of All Nations. [inferred]

## 9. Sound

Harps, lyres, double pipes and drums are shown in the art of the Near East; little is known of Persian court music. [inferred; a lyre, a double pipe and a drone stand in]

# Lhasa (the Potala), 1694 — dossier

Settlement id: `lhasa` · Style: `tibetan` · Region: Tibet (the Lhasa valley) · Code: `src/settlements/lhasa/`
Status of this dossier: L1–L2 (Marpo Ri and the Potala on it — the White Palace east and west, the Red Palace rising in the middle, the yellow Deyang Shar court, the battered walls with their rows of windows and dark parapet friezes, the gilded roofs, the stairways zigzagging up the south face; Shöl village walled at the foot; Chokpori to the south-west; ground, planting, weather, music). ## 1. The real example

- **Landmark:** the Potala on Marpo Ri, about 3,700 m, 400 m east–west and 350 m north–south, sloping stone walls 3 m thick on average and 5 m at the base [stated: Wikipedia]; thirteen storeys rising 117–119 m on the hill [stated: Wikipedia]; the White Palace lived in from 1649, the Red Palace added 1690–1694 [stated: Wikipedia]; the yellow Deyang Shar court between them [stated: famous-historic-buildings]; colours of red, white and yellow from limewash [stated: cavac (Wikipedia text)]; a principal stair rising from the east, dividing halfway to the White and the Red Palaces; sun and moon towers east and west [stated: ancient-history-sites].
- **Moment shown:** 1694, the Red Palace just finished.

## 3. Orientation

Facing south over the valley, along the east–west ridge. [stated]

## 4. The style

Battered stone walls, whitewashed or limewashed red; rows of windows with black trapezoid frames; parapets with a dark red-brown frieze of tamarisk twigs (pembe); gilded roofs with upturned eaves and victory banners on the Red Palace. [stated: colours; inferred: details]

## 5. The town

Shöl: two- and three-storey whitewashed houses with flat roofs, black-framed windows, prayer-flag poles, inside a wall with corner towers. [inferred]

## 6. Ground and water

The flat valley floor; the rocky ridge and Chokpori. (`ground.js`) [inferred]

## 7. Planting

Willows and poplars on the valley floor, barley fields; bare rock and grass on the hills. [inferred]

## 8. Sky and weather

High plateau: intensely clear, deep blue sky, strong sun, cold nights. Latitude 29.7° N. [inferred]

## 9. Sound

Long horns (dungchen) and gyaling, drums, cymbals, monks' overtone chant (a drone, a voice and a reed stand in). [inferred]

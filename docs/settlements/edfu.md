## 1. The real example

- **Landmark:** the Temple of Horus at Edfu, built 237–57 BC, its pylon facing south [stated: `docs/buildings/edfu.md` and its sources].
- **The precinct:** the temple stands inside a mudbrick enclosure wall [stated: Wikipedia "Temple of Edfu"]. The birth house (mammisi) of Ptolemy VIII stands before the pylon, to the west [stated: Wikipedia "Temple of Edfu"].
- **The town:** ancient Edfu (Behdet, Apollinopolis Magna) lay on a mound beside the temple, near the west bank of the Nile [stated: Wikipedia "Edfu"].
- **Sacred lake:** temples had sacred lakes for the priests' rites [stated: Wikipedia "Sacred lake"]. Its place here is inferred.
- **Moment shown:** about 57 BC, the temple completed.

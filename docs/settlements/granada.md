## 1. The real example

- **Landmark:** the Alhambra, the palace-fortress of the Nasrid emirs on the Sabika hill above Granada. The walls run about 740 m long and 205 m wide at the broadest, with towers [stated: Wikipedia "Alhambra"]. The Court of the Myrtles, with its pool and the Comares Tower (about 45 m), dates from Yusuf I [stated]. The Court of the Lions, with its 124 slender columns and the lion fountain, dates from Muhammad V (1362–1391) [stated: Wikipedia "Court of the Lions"]. The Alcazaba with the Torre de la Vela stands at the west end [stated].
- **The setting:** the Darro valley to the north, and the Albaicín quarter climbing the hill beyond it [stated: Wikipedia "Albaicín"].
- **Moment shown:** about 1390, under Muhammad V.

## 3. Orientation

The Court of the Myrtles runs north–south toward the Comares Tower; the hill runs east–west. [stated]

## 4. The style

Red rammed earth and brick walls and towers, crenellated; plain-walled palaces round courts; slender marble columns; tiled roofs; whitewashed houses. [stated: materials; inferred: details]

## 5. The town

About 1,000 houses in the Albaicín and below the hill. [inferred]

## 6. Ground and water

Red earth; elm woods on the slopes; cypresses, myrtle; poplars by the Darro; olives beyond. [inferred]

## 7. Sky and climate

Clear and bright; a little cloud. [inferred]

## 8. Paths

From the south up onto the hill, into the Court of the Myrtles, to the Court of the Lions. [inferred]

## 9. Sound

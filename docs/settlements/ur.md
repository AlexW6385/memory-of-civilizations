# Ur of the Third Dynasty, c. 2050 BC — dossier

Settlement id: `ur` · Style: `sumerian` · Region: Mesopotamia (the lower Euphrates) · Code: `src/settlements/ur/`
Status of this dossier: L1–L2 (the Ziggurat of Ur-Nammu on its terrace — three stages, the three stairways meeting at the gatehouse, the shrine on top; the terrace's enclosure; the Court of Nanna; the Giparu and the palace E-hursag inside the temenos wall; a quarter of courtyard houses on winding lanes; the harbour on the west with reed boats; date palms; ground, weather, music). L3 recorded for later: the Royal Cemetery's mausolea, the Edublalmah gate, the city rampart and its oval wall, the canals, the brick stamps and bitumen, interiors, people.

Tags: **[stated]** by a cited source; **[inferred]** our reasoning, written next to it.

## 1. The real example

- **Landmark:** the Great Ziggurat (E-temen-ni-gur), built by Ur-Nammu (2112–2095 BC), completed by Shulgi [stated: odysseyadventures; Studio Matrx].
- **Moment shown:** about 2050 BC, under Shulgi.

## 2. Layout of the settlement (radius 380 m)

| Part | Where (m, +x east, +z south) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ziggurat | on its terrace in the temenos | base about 64 × 46 m, three stages, 21–30 m high [stated: Smarthistory; archaeologymag]; the lowest stage about 15 m high and sloping inwards [stated: odysseyadventures; stevewatson] (11 m by Woolley's surviving remains [stated: Collège de France]); mud-brick core with a skin of baked brick set in bitumen [stated: Collège de France; islamic-architecture] |
| Stairways | three of about 100 steps on the front face, one projecting, two leaning on the wall, meeting at a gatehouse; one stair continues to the second stage | [stated: Studio Matrx; Smarthistory; stevewatson] |
| Terrace and court | the ziggurat on a raised terrace with its own walled compound; the Court of Nanna in front | [stated: odysseyadventures] |
| Temenos | the sacred precinct with the Giparu and the palace E-hursag | the palace one of three well-preserved Ur III structures [stated: Madain Project] |
| Houses | courtyard houses on lanes outside the temenos | [inferred from Woolley's later residential quarters] |
| Harbour | on the west, toward the Euphrates | [inferred: Ur had harbours on its west and north] |

Ours: the ziggurat's front faces our north (the real one faces north-east, its corners to the cardinal points) — the precinct turned 45° to keep it square to our grid; recorded as a choice. Heights: stages of 15, 7 and 5 m and a 4 m shrine, about 31 m. [inferred]

## 3. Orientation

Corners to the cardinal points, front to the north-east [stated: Smarthistory: "oriented to true north" / llmpedia: corners to cardinal points] — ours turned (above).

## 4. The style

Mud brick with baked-brick facing, the walls broken by shallow buttresses and recesses; flat roofs; bitumen; reed and palm-trunk timber. [stated: brick and bitumen; inferred: the rest]

## 5. The town

Courtyard houses of one and two storeys, plastered mud brick, blank to the lanes, rooms round an open court. [inferred]

## 6. Ground and water

The flat alluvial plain; the harbour basin on the west with water about a metre below its quay. (`ground.js`) [inferred]

## 7. Planting

Date palms in groves and along the water; reeds at the harbour; little else on the dry ground. [inferred]

## 8. Sky and weather

Hot and dry, dust haze, clear desert nights. Latitude 31° N. [inferred]

## 9. Sound

The lyre and the harp, a reed pipe, frame drums (a plucked string, the ney and a reed organ stand in). [inferred]
